OUR AGRICULTURAL VISION

Agriculture and Production

Growing Today for a Better Tomorrow

Agriculture is at the heart of LIKATI AGROTECH. We aim to promote sustainable farming, improve agricultural production and explore new opportunities for local communities in Likati.

Our Main Agricultural Crops

We focus on three important crops with potential for agricultural development and local processing.

Groundnuts

Groundnuts are an important food and commercial crop. We aim to explore improved cultivation methods and opportunities for processing groundnut products.

Rice Production

Rice is an important food crop with potential to support food security. We aim to explore better farming practices and improved production methods.

Oil Palm

Oil palm offers opportunities for local processing and value addition. Our vision includes exploring improved palm oil production methods.

Agricultural Processing and Value Addition

We believe that agriculture should go beyond crop production. Local processing can help create more value, reduce post-harvest losses and support economic development.

Groundnut Processing

Exploring opportunities to transform groundnuts into products such as groundnut paste and cooking oil.

Rice Processing

Exploring better methods of rice cleaning, drying, milling, packaging and storage.

Palm Oil Processing

Exploring improved methods of extracting, processing and storing palm oil locally.

Our Commitment to Agricultural Development

Our long-term goal is to explore a modern agricultural approach that connects production, processing, technology and market opportunities to support the development of Likati.
