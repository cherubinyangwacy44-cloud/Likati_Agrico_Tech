WHO WE ARE

About LIKATI AGROTECH

Building a Better Future Through Agriculture

LIKATI AGROTECH is an agricultural development initiative inspired by the agricultural heritage of Likati in the Democratic Republic of Congo. Our vision is to combine agriculture, technology and innovation to support sustainable development and create opportunities for local communities.

Our Story

Our story begins in Likati, where our family has a history connected to agriculture. In the past, the land supported agricultural activities such as groundnut cultivation, rice production and oil palm processing.

However, challenges such as limited technology, transportation difficulties and a lack of modern facilities affected agricultural development.

Today, we are inspired to explore new ways of transforming this agricultural heritage through modern farming methods, technology, local processing and better market opportunities.

Our Vision

To transform Likati into a modern agricultural center that inspires agricultural development and innovation in the Democratic Republic of Congo and across Africa.

Our Mission

To promote productive and sustainable agriculture by combining modern farming methods, technology, local processing and better market opportunities.

Our Objectives

Our main objectives are to support agricultural transformation and community development.

Modern Agriculture

Explore modern farming methods to improve agricultural productivity in Likati.

Crop Development

Promote the cultivation of groundnuts, rice and oil palm.

Technology and Innovation

Explore digital tools and modern technology to improve agricultural management.

Local Processing

Explore local processing solutions to reduce post-harvest losses and increase product value.

Community Development

Create opportunities for local communities through agricultural activities.

Market Access

Explore better storage, transportation and connections between farmers and buyers.
